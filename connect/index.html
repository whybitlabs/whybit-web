Connect

a one-hand remote for lun-e. an esp32-c3 glyph board, a thumbstick and a 0.96″ oled — in the same oled case the rover wears — talking to the rover over esp-now.

/ 01 — what's inside

one stick, one screen, one rover.

01esp32-c3 glyph board

the same chip family as lun-e, on a ready-made glyph c3 board with its own usb-c.

02one thumbstick

an analog stick under your thumb — push to drive, lean to steer.

03the same oled case

the 0.96″ oled sits in the snap-fit case from lun-e. its wires drop through a slot in the shell.

04esp-now link

pair once — hold the stick for 3 s — then it talks straight to the rover. no router, no app.

05printed shell

a black shell, an inner plate for the board and an orange end cap — all printable.

06made to match

black and whybit orange, same as the rover it drives.
