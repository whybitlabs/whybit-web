/ questions
do i pay now?

no. reserving is free. you pay only once yours is ready to ship.

when does it ship?

the boards are ordered. we'll email a shipping date to everyone who reserved.

do i need connect?

no. lun-e rc drives from your phone's browser at whybit.in/drive. connect is a real thumbstick for when you want one — it comes as an add-on with the rover.

can i change my order?

yes — reply to our email or get in touch any time before you pay.

is it open source?

every file — board, firmware and print parts — is on the lun-e and connect pages.
