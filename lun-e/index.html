Lun-E

a palm-sized tracked rover on its own esp32-c3 board. two n20 gear motors, printed chain tracks, esp-now control — and it still looks back at you with animated oled eyes.

01its own board

a custom esp32-c3 pcb replaces the dev board, modules and wires of the first build.

02geared tracks

two n20 gear motors on a drv8833 drive 8-tooth sprockets and printed 10.67 mm chain.

a 0.96″ oled in a snap-fit case gives it a face — and the eyes are drawn in code.

04two ways to drive

the connect controller over esp-now, or your phone over bluetooth — no router either way.

05one usb-c port

charges the cell and programs the chip through the same socket.

06sleeps like it means it

hold the button and its eyes droop shut before it drops into deep sleep. press it and they blink awake.

/ 02 — how it drives

two tracks, four moves.

there is no steering mechanism. the difference in speed between the left and right track is the steering. each side has one n20 gear motor turning an 8-tooth sprocket; the far end of the track rides on a freewheel spinning on a 625-2z bearing. fourteen printed links of 10.67 mm chain wrap the two — an exact fit, no slack.

drive
n20 → 8-tooth sprocket

one motor per side, on diagonally opposite corners. the sprocket presses straight onto the d-shaft.

idler
freewheel on a 625-2z

the bearing sits on a 5 mm stub moulded into the base; the freewheel's closed face hides it from outside.

track
10.67 mm printed chain

14 links per side. sprocket teeth drop into the window of each link; the links hinge on printed pins.

/ 03 — the board

one board. no more wiring.

everything that was a module in v1 now lives on one 26 × 42 mm, two-layer board: the esp32-c3, the motor driver, the charger and the 3.3 v regulator.

  • brainesp32-c3-mini-1 · wi-fi + ble
  • motorsdrv8833 dual h-bridge
  • powertp4054 charger · rt9080 ldo
  • usbvertical usb-c · charge + program

/ 05 — drive it

a stick in your hand, or your phone.

one firmware listens for both. connect talks esp-now; your phone talks bluetooth from a web page — nothing to install. both can be on at once: whoever moves first drives, and if the driver goes quiet for 0.3 s the tracks stop.

  • esp-nowpaired to one connect · 50 hz
  • bluetoothweb bluetooth · chrome on android / desktop
  • eyes6 moods · they blink and look where you steer
  • buttonclick for a new look · double-click pair · hold to sleep
