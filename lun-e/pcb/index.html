/ lun-e rc — the board

the board

the dev board, charger module, booster and switch from the first build are now one 26 × 42 mm, two-layer pcb. usb-c charges the cell and programs the chip; the cell feeds the motor driver and a 3.3 v ldo directly.

gerbers + step → bom ↓ pinout ↓
brain
esp32-c3-mini-1-h4 · risc-v, wi-fi + ble, 4 mb flash
motors
drv8833 dual h-bridge → 2× n20 gear motors
charging
tp4054 · 500 mA from usb-c · red led while charging
3.3 v rail
rt9080 ldo · 600 mA, 2 µA quiescent
cell
1s lipo, 300 mAh, protected · soldered to pads underneath
usb
vertical through-hole usb-c · charge + program
face
0.96″ ssd1306 oled on a 4-pin i2c header
input
one button · wakes the board from deep sleep
board
26 × 42 mm · 2 layers · 1.6 mm
power, in order
  1. 01usb-c 5 v → tp4054 charger
  2. 02tp4054 → 1s lipo (protected cell)
  3. 03cell → drv8833 motor supply
  4. 04cell → rt9080 → 3.3 v
  5. 053.3 v → esp32-c3, oled

/ 02 — pinout

what every gpio does.

gpio 2 and 8 are strapping pins, so their 10k pull-ups double as the oled's i2c pull-ups. close jumper jp1 to hold gpio 9 low and boot into download mode.

gpiofunction
0motor a · ain2
1free
2oled i2c scl · strap pull-up
3free
4motor a · ain1
5button (active low, deep-sleep wake)
6 / 7motor b · bin1 / bin2
8oled i2c sda · strap pull-up
9strap · jp1 = download mode
10motor sleep (high = motors on)
18 / 19usb d− / d+
20free
21free (boot-log tx)
/ 03 — before you solder

things that will bite you.

hand-soldered driver

the drv8833 is fitted by hand: tssop-16 at 0.65 mm pitch. flux and a fine tip help.

cell first

connect the battery before you charge or program — the board runs from the cell.

protected cell only

there is no battery protection on the board; use a 1s lipo with its own protection.

first flash

a blank chip needs jp1 shorted while you plug in usb-c. after that, it flashes straight over usb.
