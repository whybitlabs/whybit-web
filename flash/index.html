/ flash — firmware from the browser

Flash

plug a lun-e or a connect into your computer with usb-c, pick it below and press flash. no arduino ide, no platformio — the browser does it.

web serial chrome / edge keeps your settings ~30 s
this browser can't talk to usb serial ports. open this page in chrome or edge on a computer — windows, mac, linux or chromeos. phones and safari / firefox can't flash.

/ 02 — how it goes
01plug it in

usb-c from the board to your computer. use a data cable — some cables only charge.

02press flash

pick the port in the browser's list — usually “usb jtag/serial debug unit”.

03install

leave erase device unticked: the rover keeps its number, pairing, motor direction and trim.

04watch it wake

it restarts with the wake-up eyes. do the next one — and the connect, so they match.

/ 03 — if it won't connect
  • blank boarda brand-new lun-e rc: short jp1 while you plug in usb-c, flash, then remove jp1.
  • glyph boardslun-e glyph or connect not found: hold boot while you plug it in, then press flash again.
  • linuxno ports listed: sudo usermod -aG dialout $USER, then log out and back in.
  • port busyclose the serial monitor, platformio or another tab that has the board open.
  • acting strangeflash again with erase device ticked — that also clears its number and pairing.
